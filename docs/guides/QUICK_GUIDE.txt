│  🎮 HOW TO USE                                                       │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

  1️⃣  Click "▶ Start Camera" (Green button)
      → Webcam activates and shows video feed

  2️⃣  Position your face in front of camera
      → Green box appears around your face
      → Age range shows above face
      → Gender shows below age

  3️⃣  Click "📷 Capture Photo" (Blue button)
      → Saves current frame to captures/ folder

  4️⃣  Click "⏸ Stop Camera" (Red button)
      → Pauses video feed

  5️⃣  Click "✖ Exit" (Gray button)
      → Closes application
